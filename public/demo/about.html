Our Story

Founded in 2018, Apex Global Technology began with a simple idea: make enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure and localization accessible to businesses of every size. Today we serve over 2,400 clients across 48 countries, processing millions of translations every day.

Our platform combines autonomous translation engines with human review workflows, ensuring that every word published to your website meets the highest quality standards. We believe language should never be a barrier to global growth.

From our headquarters in San Francisco, our team of 120 engineers, linguists, and support specialists works around the clock to deliver reliable, secure, and scalable solutions.
